## 14. Notes on Rejection Risk

- An O-1A denial does **not** automatically affect B1/B2 renewal — they are separate visa categories assessed independently.
- An EB-1A (immigrant) petition filing may raise **immigrant intent** questions during B1/B2 renewal — discuss strategy with lawyer before filing EB-1A while still relying on B1/B2.
- Any denial can be appealed via motion to reconsider, or refiled with stronger evidence.
- Note: As of January 2026, Bangladeshi nationals applying for B1/B2 are subject to a bond of up to $15,000. Discuss with lawyer.
- **Do not file before the profile is strong enough.** A premature filing that is denied is avoidable. Quality of evidence and lawyer framing matters significantly.
